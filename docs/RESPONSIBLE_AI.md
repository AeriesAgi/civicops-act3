# Responsible AI and location boundaries

These are enforced product boundaries for CivicOps, including future municipal and public-safety expansion. They cannot be relaxed through provider configuration, a model prompt or a demo label. Repository evidence establishes local behavior with synthetic data; it does not establish a municipal lawful basis, production approval or live detector accuracy.

A generic detector label, unknown property or an externally supplied “risk score” does not bypass it. Model text cannot select an arbitrary crew, approve a hazard or mutate the dispatch lifecycle.

## Data and operating limits

The demo marks every exercise municipality/ward/crew/hazard as **SYNTHETIC** and provider behavior as **SIMULATED**. Real cases receive coordinates only through explicit report sharing. Geofencing is a geometric association, not verification of a resident address, service jurisdiction or dispatch safety. Heatmap cells express operational case/hazard volume; they are not neighbourhood safety rankings, crime predictions or person-location feeds.

Geo storage contains one latest synthetic point per seeded crew. Seeded points expire after 24 hours; hazards expire after seven days; report coordinates expire after 30 days. The separate crew store accepts pings only against current server-issued shift/consent records. Defaults are a 60-second sampling interval, 100-metre accuracy limit, 30-day raw retention and a 12-hour maximum shift. Sampling is bounded to 15–600 seconds; retention is configurable to 1–30 days. Current supervisor positions disappear at end/withdrawal/expiry, independently of raw-history retention. Workers can inspect/export their own retained history. Purge aggregates retain sample counts per tenant/day without worker association; priority still applies only to incidents/hazards.

The tenant-aware automatic purge runs with defaults, and operational reads exclude expired points. Local deletion does not wipe old storage media, historical audit, backups, prior exports or provider-held records. Existing case/contact retention and those residual boundaries are documented in [POPIA engineering controls](privacy/POPIA.md). Municipal Information Officer approval, legal holds, lawful basis and the complete retention schedule remain owner-pending. Crew job photos always lose metadata on the server, including GPS, even when a client recorded an opt-in choice. No detector/video frames enter that evidence endpoint. The af/zu resources are starter translations requiring native-speaker review.

Permitted crew suggestion factors are department, availability, required skills, valid certification evidence, position freshness and straight-line distance. Certificates/skills must still be verified by municipal staff; synthetic certificates are exercise data. There is no automated dispatch, arrival guarantee or emergency-response claim. Municipal police, Fire, EMS and Disaster Management integration and their training remain future work.

## Voice, SMS and WhatsApp enforcement

[Closed normalized inbound and admin DTOs](../products/civicops/Models/Channels.cs) accept reviewed service information, optional volunteered report location and opaque provider media IDs. Native voice adapters retain only redacted structured text/summary; WhatsApp audio/video is routed to human review without storing media IDs or bytes. CivicOps never downloads provider media, records calls, stores camera frames, extracts voiceprints or identifies people. [Adapter guards](../tests/CivicOps.Tests/ChannelAdapterTests.cs), [pipeline/schema guards](../tests/CivicOps.Tests/ChannelPipelineTests.cs) and the existing all-model/all-schema biometric scanner enforce these limits. Unknown SIP and normalized fields fail closed.

[ChannelStore](../products/civicops/Services/ChannelStore.cs) encrypts contacts with tenant-bound AES-GCM, keys number/content hashes, limits readable contact labels and audits staff access without narrative/coordinate payloads. A changed or missing custody key prevents processing. Current-purpose notice and explicit resident consent gate case processing; STOP/STOPALL suppress numbers independently of retained conversations, and START re-enables only its channel. Human sends require trusted dispatch permission, current versions, a reason and durable operation ID. Suppression blocks the normalized and legacy callback paths before provider construction. SMS and WhatsApp panic phrases record a control-room SOS alert for a named human. Voice SOS supplies only the specified emergency numbers and does not open that alert. A channel request never approves, assigns, calls or dispatches.

Volunteered WhatsApp points are validated/geofenced without text-to-coordinate inference. A location cannot silently move an approved/assigned operational case: further information requires a separately pending human decision. Existing worker shift/consent tests and location collection rules remain unchanged. Channel contacts/messages and pending report/send text have bounded automatic purge; suppression and minimal receipt/audit metadata persist under documented purpose limits. [Channel retention and residual-data boundaries](CHANNELS.md#data-audit-and-limits) require municipal Information Officer review, provider agreements and private backup/key custody. en/af/zu notices/templates are versioned starter resources requiring owner/native-speaker approval.

## Out of scope by design

- Recognition or identification of faces, bodies, gait, voiceprints, fingerprints, iris/retina or biometric embeddings.
- Licence plates connected to a person or identity database; person/vehicle movement dossiers.
- Person/neighbourhood suspicion, threat, criminality or predicted-crime scoring; predictive policing.
- Covert cameras, stored camera frames, automatic recording, hidden location collection or continuous worker tracking.
- Automated police interventions, safety decisions, approvals, dispatches or external notifications.

Object-only detection adapters remain future engineering. [Round-10 crew clients](CREW_APP.md) implement explicit shifts, revocable consent, visible indicators and bounded location retention. Native build/runtime evidence is distinguished in [Android build notes](ANDROID_BUILD.md); a local build does not prove field-device behavior. The round-12 SOS workflow is a tested local synthetic alert contract. Escalation and false-alarm notices are simulated audit records. No responder notification, emergency call, provider send, person score or automatic dispatch occurs. A production deployment must enforce access reviews, encrypted private storage, monitoring and approved municipal notices; [enterprise readiness](ENTERPRISE_READINESS.md) records the remaining acceptance work.

# Synthetic municipal service FAQ

Culltron CivicOps initially proves the workflow inside Culltron support. This knowledge is synthetic and does not claim a municipal contract or verified local service coverage.

Report what happened and a resident-supplied area or landmark. Contact is optional; email or a voice callback requires explicit consent. Never share passwords, identity numbers or payment information.

CivicOps records service reports and provides a case reference. It does not replace emergency services. For immediate danger contact 10111 (police), 10177 (ambulance/fire), or 112 from a mobile. Move away from live wires, fire and floodwater if safe.

English and Afrikaans presets are provided. A dispatcher can review uncertain language or translation. No SMS or messaging channel is offered.

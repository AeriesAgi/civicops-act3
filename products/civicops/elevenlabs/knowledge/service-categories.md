# Service categories

- WATER: burst pipes, water leaks and sanitation → Water and Sanitation.
- ROADS: potholes, road hazards, stormwater and blocked drains → Roads and Stormwater.
- ELECTRICITY: power outages, streetlights and electrical hazards → Electricity.
- WASTE: rubbish collection and illegal dumping → Waste Management.
- GENERAL: uncertain or other service reports → Public Works for human review.

Danger always raises urgency. A possible duplicate is flagged for human review and is never merged automatically. Department routing is a recommendation until reviewed.

YOUR DEVICE, YOUR CHOICES

Settings & privacy

Language

English is complete. Afrikaans and isiZulu use partial, machine-drafted labels with English fallback; native-speaker and municipal notice review are pending.

Use your browser's Install app or Add to Home Screen menu when available. HTTPS or localhost is required.

My location history

Only your own retained on-shift pings are available here. Access and export are audited. Supervisors cannot use this screen to inspect another worker.

Offline data on this device

Assigned jobs are cached for at most 24 hours. Unsent job notes, status changes and metadata-stripped photos expire after 7 days. Private data is scoped to your municipality and worker and cleared at sign-out or loss of authorisation. No token or worker location ping enters IndexedDB or the service worker. Paired access uses tab-scoped sessionStorage, clears on sign-out/auth loss/expiry, and is verified online before sync. Use a device lock and your municipality's MDM policy.

Work evidence is for completing approved jobs. Avoid names, identity numbers, people and sensitive personal details in notes and photos. Photos are re-encoded on this device and always omit EXIF/GPS metadata; this web fallback does not preserve per-photo GPS metadata.

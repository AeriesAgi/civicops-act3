CULLTRON CIVICOPS · MUNICIPAL OPERATIONS

Every resident report, routed to the right crew. A person approves every dispatch.

CivicOps is an accountable operations platform for South African municipalities. Voice, chat, SMS and WhatsApp reports become one tracked case, AI agents help with triage, and dispatchers, agencies and auditors share one hash-chained timeline.

Try it as a residentTry it as municipal staffSource on GitHub ↗

Public demo · synthetic data only. Every municipality, person, case, crew position and camera event here is fictional, and voice, ticketing and messaging providers are simulated. Please don't enter real personal details.

How it works

  1. 1

    A resident reports

    Burst pipe, downed line or flooding, by web, voice, SMS or WhatsApp, in English, Afrikaans or isiZulu.

  2. 2

    Agents triage

    Bounded agents pull out the location, hazards and department, then recommend a qualified crew. Each step shows its reasoning.

  3. 3

    A person decides

    A dispatcher approves, edits or rejects. Nothing gets dispatched without a named human.

  4. 4

    Crews respond and it's all on record

    Crews get the job with safety checks. Every action is added to an append-only, verifiable audit chain.

ONE FRONT DOOR

Residents get one place to report and follow up

Residents get a privacy notice, consent controls and a case reference they can check later. Reports from voice, SMS and WhatsApp land in the same unified inbox.

HUMAN-APPROVED DISPATCH

Dispatchers see the recommendation and make the call

The case queue, workflow outcomes, crew qualifications and certifications all sit in one workspace. AI suggests. Your staff decide.

LIVE OPERATIONS MAP

One municipal picture

Wards, cases, hazards, assets and crews on a self-hosted map, with an incident heatmap and a fully keyboard-accessible feature list.

MULTI-AGENCY

One incident, every agency

Metro Police, Fire, EMS, Disaster Management, Traffic and four utilities share incidents and mutual-aid requests, each with their own roles. Sensitive police incidents stay restricted to that agency.

SCENE EVENTS ONLY

Camera alerts that respect privacy

The simulated camera feed reports scene events like flooding, debris or a blocked lane. It never identifies faces or number plates, and a person confirms every event before anything happens.

RESPONSIBLE AI · SAFETY

Built to be accountable from day one

AI here is a bounded assistant, not the decision-maker.

  • Human in the loop

    Every dispatch, mutual-aid request and camera event needs a named person to approve it.

  • Never a replacement for emergency numbers

    SOS alerts the municipal control room only. For life-threatening danger, call 10111, 10177 or 112.

  • Built for POPIA privacy

    Purpose-limited consent, masked contact details and access, correction and anonymisation requests.

  • Tamper-evident audit

    Each event links to the previous one with SHA-256, and staff can verify the whole chain.

  • Locked down by default

    A strict Content Security Policy, self-hosted assets, tenant isolation and role-based access.

  • Honest about simulation

    Simulated providers and synthetic data are labelled clearly throughout the demo.
